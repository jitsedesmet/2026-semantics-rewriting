With the finalization of the [RDF 1.2](cite:cites rdf-1-2) and [SPARQL 1.2 specification](cite:cites sparql-1-2) both data publishers and
queriers will get access to the newly introduced triple term.
RDF 1.2 extends the existing [RDF 1.1](cite:cites spec:rdf-1-1) specification with the addition of triple terms,
which are terms allowed in the object position of a triple that are again a triple.
Allowing for an infinitely deep nesting of triples within the objects of other triples.
This idea is a derivative of what [RDF and SPARQL star](cite:cites hartig2017foundations) introduced over a decade ago.
Additionally, a draft spec is being created to cover [RDF 1.2 interoperability](cite:cites spec:rdf-1-2-interop).
In this work, we present a generic approach to bridge between RDF 1.1 and RDF 1.2.
