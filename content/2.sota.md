## Related work
{:#related-work}

Even-though imperative rewriting algorithms could suffice to cover the use-case of
query rewriting between SPARQL 1.2 and SPARQL 1.1 under a given reification scheme,
a generic and declarative approach introduces several advantages:
<!-- -->
1. adaptability to unknown reification scheme, 
2. interpretability of the declarative nature, and
3. reuse of existing skills
<!-- -->
To this end, we will not discuss an imperative algorithm that covers just our bounded use-case,
but instead consult the broader data integration literature.

**Global-as-View**
In the [tradition data integration literature](cite:cites lenzerini2002data), data integration is achieved though describing mappings.
These napping can either:
<!-- -->
1. Describe global view given your local sources, or
2. describe your local sources given the global view.
