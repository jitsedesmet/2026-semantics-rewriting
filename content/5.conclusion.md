## Conclusion
{:#conclusion}

<!--
Show ongoing work. But also open the discussion on required language extensions such as CONSTRUCT GRAPH, term template syntactic sugar, recursive view definitions etc
-->

This work demonstrates a preliminary implementation for
a generic framework to cover RDF 1.2 interoperability through query rewriting.  
To this end, we opt for a declarative approach rather than an imperative rewriting algorithm
since this allows
<!-- -->
1. extensibility in unforeseen cases,
2. keeps transformations interpretable, and
3. reuses existing SPARQL skills.
<!-- -->
Our approach uses GAV-mappings expressed as SPARQL CONSTRUCT queries
and performs query rewriting through unfolding of the mappings.
By keeping the mapping declarations within the SPARQL query language,
our approach can rewrite the query to valid SPARQL,
and shortcomings in the expressivity of the queries
can spark discussion on langauge extensions and
required features to be implemented in the maintenance mode of the SPARQL working group.
Since the demonstration is part of ongoing work, optimizations as well as required langauge extensions are seen as future work for now.
Our goal is to draw attention to the GAV-mappings,
showcase their usefulness,
and open discussion desired language features.
