## Resources used for creation

> On-going/Completed work

>   - New publicly available datasets
>    - Novel algorithms or tools


> Review Criteria
> Submissions must clearly demonstrate their relevance to the aforementioned topics of SEMANTiCS 2026. Reviews will
> evaluate:
>    - Relevance to the topics of interest
>    - Originality and novelty of the contribution
>    - Significance and potential impact
>    - Topicality, i.e., the timeliness and current relevance of the work
>    - Clarity and quality of presentation


> Links: Authors should ensure that the demoed version is available online for inspection. If this is not possible,
> authors are encouraged to submit a link to a video of their system (max 2 minutes duration). This will positively
> support the overall assessment of the contribution.
> Ethics and Environmental footprint: How does the work described in your submission relate to ethical issues and how does
> it affect the environment?
> Declaration on Generative AI: Submissions must adhere to the fair use of Large Language Models. Please refer to the
> SEMANTiCS full policy for more details: llm-policy, and must provide a declaration as stated in CEUR’s GenAI Policy.


> Demos are expected to submit a video of their work accompanied by rich metadata on video description, captions, and
> transcripts. For recording your video, kindly consider the guidelines provided by WAI
> at https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/av-content/#video . You can find information about how to formulate video captions
> at https://www.w3.org/WAI/perspective-videos/captions/, video transcripts
> at https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/transcripts/, and video descriptions at https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/description/.
